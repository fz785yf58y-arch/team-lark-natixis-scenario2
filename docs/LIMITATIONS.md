# Evidence and residual risks

- Historical source inputs end17 September2026. Research and publication occur
  later; later webpages cannot be backdated as market information at the cutoff.
- The equity pool excludes some historical exits and lacks complete historical
  shares/industry/Asian financial records. Partial PIT features do not remove
  that bias. The shortlist and every backtest partition have already been seen;
  chronological holdouts are not blind out-of-sample evidence.
- Frozen note inputs allow computational replay but are not independent raw
  source retrieval. Future provider revisions can prevent exact hash matching.
- No complete5Y basket implied-volatility surface, issuer credit curve or
  dealer spread is supplied. Realized-vol options and short public vol indices
  are proxies. Modeled protection does not create a signed legal guarantee.
- FICC actual six-bond daily histories are missing; CEMB and fitted zero curves
  are proxies. Terminal bond-sale values and reinvestment assumptions matter.
- Asian versus US versus UTC closes are not synchronous executable prices.
  ETF price histories contain vehicle changes, futures roll, expenses and
  premiums/discounts. A different contractual underlying requires a new run.
- Several reference stocks/commodities do not imply several note-bank issuers.
  Proposed limits require at least3independent bank-parent groups and no more
  than1/3funded note face per group; this is a design policy, not a historical
  multi-bank issuance dataset. Default is excluded from the main NAV path.
- VUSXX is a variable-rate fund with dealing, access and receiving-bank timing;
  smoothing distributions to daily accrual is approximate accounting, not an
  observed daily security-price series or a guaranteed payment clock.
- $60m Treasury face is a redemption target, not $60m purchase cost. Treasury
  principal must be retained for the33-day maturity/horizon bridge. Nominal
  protection does not protect purchasing power or early-sale market value.
- Source URLs, public IDs and turnover screens do not establish Bloomberg
  verification, issue-date legal access, full sanctions clearance, signed terms
  or executable issuer quotations. Quotes in this package are indicative model
  assumptions. Expensive options must reduce participation/add a cap or fund
  more budget; they cannot silently remove stated protection.
- No historical or hybrid number guarantees future5Y or20Y returns. Extreme
  maturity payoffs test algebra, not probabilities.ES99has26observations and
  cannot measure remote default likelihood. No global-optimality claim is made.
